# Code of conduct

`ctxscope` welcomes people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Help keep technical discussions focused and accessible.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact information on the
maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private channel is available, open an issue
requesting private contact without including sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily suspend,
or permanently exclude participants whose behavior harms the community. Reports
will be handled as privately as practical.
